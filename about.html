Our Story

From a small Accra startup to Ghana's #1 online fitness destination

BUILT FOR GHANAIAN FITNESS

FitZone GH was founded in 2019 by Kofi Mensah and Ama Darko — two personal trainers from Accra who were frustrated by the lack of quality, affordable gym equipment available locally. Importing equipment themselves was expensive and unreliable, so they decided to build a better solution.

Starting with just a website, a WhatsApp number and a rented storage unit in Tema, FitZone GH rapidly grew through word-of-mouth referrals from the fitness community. By 2021, they had served over 2,000 customers and expanded their product range to include premium international supplement brands.

Today, FitZone GH operates a modern e-commerce platform, a physical showroom in Accra Mall and a nationwide delivery network reaching all 16 regions of Ghana. Our mission remains simple: make world-class fitness accessible to every Ghanaian.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do at FitZone GH.

🎯

Authenticity

Every product we sell is genuine. We source directly from certified manufacturers and distributors, protecting our customers from counterfeits.
